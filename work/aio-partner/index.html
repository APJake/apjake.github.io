AiO Partner is the companion app to AiO eSports, built for the teams and the talent on the other side of the fan flow. Same backend, different audience.

  • Team self-service
  • Talent self-service
  • Profile editing
  • Content publishing

  1. 01

    1 model

    shared with the fan app

    Reuses the same backend as AiO eSports

    The same content model that powers the fan app's profile pages drives this one in reverse — teams and talent write to it, fans read from it. Auth and roles are what separates the two clients.

  2. 02

    2 audiences

    teams · talent

    Teams and talent in one app

    Two user kinds, one app. Teams manage rosters and updates; casters and players manage their individual profiles. The same app is the right tool because the underlying actions are the same.

  3. 03

    1 store

    Android

    Native Android, no Flutter

    Shipped as a native Android app alongside AiO eSports. Shared patterns, shared tooling, shared release process.

Same freelance engagement as AiO eSports; the two apps were always designed to ship together.
