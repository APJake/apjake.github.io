25:Td2c,<p>Exploding Kittens is a card game for 2 to 5 players. It's basically Russian
roulette with cats. You take turns drawing cards, and whoever draws an
Exploding Kitten without a way to defuse it is out. The last player still
standing wins. A game takes about 15 minutes.</p>
<h2>Setup</h2>
<ol>
<li>Take all the <strong>Exploding Kittens</strong> and <strong>Defuse</strong> cards out of the deck.</li>
<li>Shuffle the rest and deal <strong>7 cards</strong> to each player.</li>
<li>Give every player <strong>1 Defuse</strong>, so each hand starts with 8 cards.</li>
<li>Shuffle the leftover Defuses back into the deck. With 2 or 3 players,
put back only 2 of them.</li>
<li>Shuffle <strong>one fewer Exploding Kitten than there are players</strong> into the
deck. With 4 players, that's 3 kittens. Put any extra kittens back in
the box.</li>
<li>Put the deck face down in the middle. That's the draw pile.</li>
</ol>
<h2>A turn</h2>
<p>On your turn you can play <strong>as many cards as you like</strong>, one at a time, or
none at all. Then you <strong>end your turn by drawing one card</strong> from the draw
pile.</p>
<p>That draw is the scary part. If it's an Exploding Kitten, you have to play
a Defuse right away. Then put the kitten back into the draw pile anywhere
you like, in secret, so the other players don't know where it is. With no
Defuse, you explode and you're out.</p>
<h2>What the cards do</h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Card</th>
<th>What it does</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>Defuse</strong></td>
<td>Saves you from an Exploding Kitten. Then put the kitten back anywhere in the deck.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Skip</strong></td>
<td>End your turn without drawing.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Attack</strong></td>
<td>End your turn without drawing. The next player takes <strong>2 turns</strong> in a row.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>See the Future</strong></td>
<td>Look at the top 3 cards of the draw pile. Put them back in the same order.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Shuffle</strong></td>
<td>Shuffle the draw pile. Great right after someone hides a kitten.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Favor</strong></td>
<td>Pick a player. They give you a card of their choice.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Nope</strong></td>
<td>Cancels any action card someone just played, except a Defuse or an Exploding Kitten. You can play it any time, even when it's not your turn, and you can Nope a Nope.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Cat cards</strong></td>
<td>Do nothing alone. Play a <strong>pair</strong> of the same cat to steal a random card from another player.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Save your Nope</strong> for an Attack aimed at you, or for someone trying to
steal your Defuse.</li>
<li><strong>See the Future + Skip</strong> is a classic combo: if a kitten is on top,
skip and let it go to the next player.</li>
<li><strong>Hide kittens where people won't expect them.</strong> Putting a kitten right
on top so the next player draws it is mean, but very effective.</li>
<li><strong>Count the kittens.</strong> You always know how many are left: one fewer
than the players still in the game. The smaller the deck, the riskier
every draw.</li>
</ul>
